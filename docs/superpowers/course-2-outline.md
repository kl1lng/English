# Course 2 outline: Work and Study in Kazakhstan (B2), for approval

Status: **proposal, no lessons written yet.** Approve, change or replace the topics; then lessons are written in batches of five, checked by `src/lessons/lessons.test.js`, and the Kazakh meanings go through the native-speaker review (`scripts/kk-review.mjs`).

Lesson shape (same as course 1): fact, 4 or more speaking questions, 6 words with Kazakh and Russian meaning and an example sentence that contains the word, 1-2 grammar topics, minimum 60 seconds (recommended 75, maximum 90). Level B2.

| Module | Lessons (working titles) | Grammar topics to practise |
|---|---|---|
| 1. City life | Getting around Astana and Almaty; Renting a flat; Shopping and services; Neighbours and community; Public holidays in the city; Your city compared with another | comparatives, present perfect, modals of advice |
| 2. Work and careers | A job interview; Your first week at work; Negotiating and disagreeing politely; Working remotely; Learning on the job; Changing careers | conditionals, reported speech, would/could for politeness |
| 3. Education and science | Choosing a university; Research and ideas; Internships and grants; Science in Kazakhstan; Studying abroad; Lifelong learning | passive voice, relative clauses, linking words |
| 4. Health and lifestyle | Visiting a doctor; Sport and fitness; Food and habits; Stress and sleep; Planning a holiday; Home and family | present perfect continuous, should/ought to, used to |
| 5. Technology and future | AI in everyday life; Digital services in Kazakhstan; Green energy; Start-ups; Culture online; Kazakhstan in 2050 | future forms, first and second conditional, cause and effect |
